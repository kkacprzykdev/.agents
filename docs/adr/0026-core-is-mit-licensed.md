# Core is MIT licensed

Core is published under the MIT license, in `LICENSE` at the store root. The goal is daily use by many developers, and `ag init` works by cloning this repository, so nobody should have to ask whether they may use, change, or share it. MIT is the license most developers and companies accept without review.

The license covers Core only. A user's profiles, their artifacts, and the third-party skills they install are their own content, under whatever terms they choose. A third-party skill keeps its own license.

## Considered Options

- **No license:** a public repository without one is "all rights reserved". Others may read it but not use, copy, or change it, which blocks the purpose of the tool.
- **A source-available license, such as FSL or BSL:** it keeps the right to sell `ag` with the author, but some companies do not allow such licenses. Paid work can still come from sponsorship, support, or separate paid additions.
- **Apache-2.0:** it adds a patent grant, which matters little for a tool of this size, and it is longer to read.
